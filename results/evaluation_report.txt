# Assignment 11 — Real-Time Voice-Controlled AI Application

## 1. Introduction

The objective of this assignment was to develop and evaluate a real-time voice-controlled AI application using Python.

The developed application is called **Claude AI Voice Tutor**. It allows users to interact with artificial intelligence through spoken questions and spoken responses.

The application uses Faster-Whisper for speech recognition, Anthropic Claude for generating responses, Piper for text-to-speech, and Silero VAD for detecting speech activity.

## 2. System Architecture

The application follows the processing pipeline:

Microphone → Silero VAD → Faster-Whisper → Claude AI → Piper TTS → Speaker

Silero VAD detects when the user begins and finishes speaking. Faster-Whisper converts the recorded speech into text. The text is sent to Claude, which generates an answer. Piper converts the answer into audio and plays it through the computer speakers.

An experimental interruption mechanism monitors microphone input during audio playback.

## 3. Response Latency Evaluation

## 6. Voice Interruption Evaluation

The application uses Silero VAD to detect speech while Claude is speaking.

During initial testing, the system repeatedly triggered interruptions shortly after audio playback began. This caused Claude's answers to stop prematurely and led to additional unintended transcriptions.

The exact cause has not yet been confirmed. Speaker feedback and unreliable recognition of short audio segments are possible contributing factors.

## 10. Conclusion

The Claude AI Voice Tutor successfully demonstrates a speech-to-text, AI-response, and text-to-speech pipeline.

Initial results confirm that the application can recognize spoken questions and generate relevant AI responses. The prototype also supports automatic speech detection and experimental interruption.

Further evaluation is required to quantify speech recognition accuracy, test network recovery, and assess the reliability of interruptions and speaker echo handling.

The most important identified improvement is preventing unintended interruptions during AI speech playback.
